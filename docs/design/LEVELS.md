# Ironoak — Level Identity Cards

Each level is designed from this card **before** greyboxing. A level must differ from every
other level on at least 4 of the 6 identity axes (target: all 6). Puzzle IDs refer to
[PUZZLES.md](PUZZLES.md). Times are for a first play.

**Standard rhythm (every level):** calm → teach → test → combine → peak → release.
**Standard collectibles (every level):** 3 secrets + 1 Druid's Trial · 1 Ogham stone (lore)
· 1 Golden torc · 1 Shrine shard (3 shards = +1 heart, so a shard is in every second
secret across the game) · coins.

## Level 1 — The Oak Road  *(POC vertical slice)*

> **One sentence:** *"A sunny forest where I learned to make the ground rise, then outran a boar stampede."*

| Axis | Value |
|---|---|
| New power | **Rootgrow** (sling is available from the start) |
| Movement rule | Normal ground, gentle slopes |
| Main challenge | Learning to read the world: puzzles first, light combat |
| Set piece | **Boar stampede**: an auto-scrolling chase where you raise roots to vault and block |
| Enemy twist | Legionary (patrols; stomp or sling-stun) |
| Look and sound | Greens and golds, dappled light, flute and harp, birdsong |

**Length:** ~10 min first play · 15–18 min with all secrets · 3 min speedrun

| Time | Beat | Puzzle / content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Story card, village road | Movement, coins, mood |
| 1:00 | Sling lesson | P1-01, P1-02 |
| 2:30 | First patrol, cracked wall | Combat basics → **Secret 1A** |
| 3:30 | Tavo's broken cart | Torc found (Rootgrow unlocked, no text) |
| 4:30 | Rootgrow lesson → test → twist | P1-03, P1-04, P1-05 |
| 6:30 | First combination | P1-06 |
| 7:30 | Root shaft (optional) | **Druid's Trial: P1-T** |
| 8:00 | Boar stampede | P1-07 (chase) |
| 9:30 | Friendly banner, results | Story hook: "they took them to the river" |

**Secrets**
- **1A Cracked Wall** *(teaches "look for cracks")*: sling the cracked stones beside the first patrol. Reward: Ogham stone *"The Smiths' Oath"*.
- **1B Canopy Nest**: a root column climbs past the top of the screen into a hidden treetop room. Reward: Golden torc.
- **1C Under the Dam**: after P1-05 drains the pool, a tunnel appears in the dry bed. Reward: shrine shard.

**Most memorable moment:** the boar stampede, with roots erupting just in time.
**Difficulty target:** nobody gets stuck longer than 60 s on the main path.

## Level 2 — The River Crossing

> **One sentence:** *"I built bridges out of branches over a rising flood, then rode the rapids."*

| Axis | Value |
|---|---|
| New power | **Branchgrow**: sideways branches from knots; a sling shot snaps them |
| Movement rule | Water: currents push you, floating logs, a raft |
| Main challenge | Timing and flow; the water level changes the puzzle |
| Set piece | **Rapids ride**: steer a raft and grow branches to dodge and deflect debris |
| Enemy twist | **Spearman**: throws arcing spears from the far bank; branches block them |
| Look and sound | Cool blues and whites, mist, rushing water, frame drums |

**Length:** ~15–20 min · 25–30 min with all secrets

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Calm | Riverbank village ruins, the first freed smith (story) |
| Teach | Branchgrow as a bridge (P2-01) |
| Test | Branch as a ramp for a crate (P2-02) |
| Twist | Snap a branch to drop it as a dam or a weight (P2-03) |
| Combine | Root + branch: lift the water level to float a raft (P2-04, P2-05) |
| Peak | Rapids ride (P2-06) |
| Release | Calm lake shore; camp fires visible in the distance |

**Secrets**
- **2A Waterfall Cave**: behind the falls, reached with a snapped-branch slide. Ogham *"The River Remembers"*.
- **2B Sunken Shrine**: lower the water with a root dam to reveal it. Shrine shard.
- **2C Heron's Perch**: a high branch chain leads off the top of the screen. Golden torc.

**Druid's Trial P2-T:** branches that snap themselves after 3 s, a timed crossing.
**Most memorable moment:** snapping your own bridge so it lands as a raft.

## Level 3 — The Night Marsh

> **One sentence:** *"In the dark fog I hid from lanterns and swung on vines over the bog."*

| Axis | Value |
|---|---|
| New power | **Vinefall**: drop a vine from ceiling knots (swing, climb, pulley weight) |
| Movement rule | Sinking mud (keep moving), darkness (limited light radius) |
| Main challenge | **Stealth**: lantern vision cones; being spotted brings reinforcements |
| Set piece | **Search line**: a row of soldiers with lanterns sweeps the marsh; hide in reeds and climb vines |
| Enemy twist | **Patrol dogs**: follow your scent trail; water or vines break the trail |
| Look and sound | Purple-black, fireflies as the light source, crickets, low drones |

**Length:** ~15–20 min · 25–30 min with all secrets

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Calm | Fireflies, first look at the dark |
| Teach | Vinefall as a climb (P3-01), then a swing (P3-02) |
| Test | Vine as a pulley counterweight (P3-03) |
| Twist | Darkness: grow roots to block lantern beams (P3-04) |
| Combine | Branch, vine and root stealth route (P3-05) |
| Peak | Search line (P3-06) |
| Release | Dawn over the marsh edge; palisade walls ahead |

**Secrets**
- **3A Firefly Grove**: follow the firefly trail off the main path. Golden torc.
- **3B Drowned Hut**: vine down a well. Ogham *"Varro's Map"* (foreshadows the war machine).
- **3C Mud Pocket**: sink on purpose in one specific bog to drop into a cavern. Shrine shard.

**Druid's Trial P3-T:** a lights-out room where you navigate only by the sound and light of a lantern you swing.
**Most memorable moment:** hanging from a vine while a lantern passes just below you.

## Level 4 — The Roman Camp

> **One sentence:** *"I turned the Romans' own iron against them and freed the prisoners."*

| Axis | Value |
|---|---|
| New power | **Lodestone**: pull or push iron objects (crates, shields, chains, counterweights) |
| Movement rule | Climbing palisades and tent ropes; pushable carts |
| Main challenge | **Rescue and escort**: freed smiths follow you and need safe paths |
| Set piece | **Wagon breakout**: free 3 prisoner wagons before the alarm horn finishes |
| Enemy twist | **Shield-bearer**: blocks all attacks from the front; Lodestone rips the shield away |
| Look and sound | Warm reds and browns, torchlight, camp chatter, brass fanfares |

**Length:** ~20 min · 30 min with all secrets

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Calm | Overlook of the camp, story about where Tavo is |
| Teach | Pull an iron crate (P4-01), push a counterweight (P4-02) |
| Test | Disarm a shield-bearer, use his shield as a platform (P4-03) |
| Twist | Escort: pick a route a smith can walk (P4-04) |
| Combine | Branch, Lodestone and sling gate puzzle (P4-05) |
| Peak | Wagon breakout (P4-06) |
| Mini-boss | **Centurion Rufus**: ends up in the river, as always |

**Secrets**
- **4A Quartermaster's Tent**: Lodestone open a locked strongbox. Golden torc.
- **4B Under the Latrines** (comic): a vine down into the tunnels. Ogham *"Letters Home"* (the legionaries' homesick letters, which humanise them).
- **4C Signal Tower Top**: chain three powers to climb it. Shrine shard.

**Druid's Trial P4-T:** magnetic chains swinging over spikes.
**Most memorable moment:** pulling a shield off a soldier mid-charge and surfing on it.

## Level 5 — The Iron Mines

> **One sentence:** *"Underground I heated and bent iron, then fled a collapsing mine."*

| Axis | Value |
|---|---|
| New power | **Forge-heat**: heat iron until it glows (it bends, melts, expands, lights fires) |
| Movement rule | Mine carts on rails, low ceilings, heat zones that drain stamina |
| Main challenge | **Escape under pressure**; heat timers on objects |
| Set piece | **The collapse**: a mine-cart escape where you heat and bend rails to switch tracks |
| Enemy twist | **Furnace guards**: armoured and immune to the sling; heat their armour to drive them off |
| Look and sound | Black and molten orange, sparks, hammer rhythms, deep percussion |

**Length:** ~20 min · 30 min with all secrets

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Calm | Smiths at forced labour; reunion with an old friend of Tavo's |
| Teach | Heat bars to bend them (P5-01), melt a lock (P5-02) |
| Test | Heat makes a bridge expand to close a gap (P5-03) |
| Twist | Heated iron plus Lodestone: pull a hot crate to light braziers (P5-04) |
| Combine | All earlier powers in one forge room (P5-05) |
| Peak | The collapse (P5-06) |
| Release | Daylight shaft; Fort Varro looms above |

**Secrets**
- **5A Abandoned Seam**: bend a bar gate to enter. Golden torc.
- **5B Tavo's Marks**: a hidden wall carving of Tavo's code. Ogham *"The Flaw in the Gear"* (sets up the final boss).
- **5C Crystal Grotto**: cool the lava with a root dam. Shrine shard.

**Druid's Trial P5-T:** a heat-timer relay where five bars must be hot at the same moment.
**Most memorable moment:** bending a rail mid-ride to switch tracks.

## Level 6 — Fort Varro

> **One sentence:** *"I climbed the fort in a storm and broke Varro's war machine using everything I'd learned."*

| Axis | Value |
|---|---|
| New power | None; **mastery of all five**, used fast and in combination |
| Movement rule | Storm wind pushes you on the walls; rain makes surfaces slippery |
| Main challenge | **Boss fights** and gauntlets |
| Set piece | **The war machine**: a multi-phase boss where each phase needs a different power |
| Enemy twist | **Ballista crews**: long-range bolts; their bolts stick in walls as temporary platforms |
| Look and sound | Grey stone, lightning flashes, full orchestral theme with Celtic instruments |

**Length:** ~20–25 min · 30–35 min with all secrets

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Calm | Storm approach; Brenna's last quiet moment |
| Gauntlet | Wall climb using all powers (P6-01 to P6-03) |
| Rescue | Free Tavo, who joins the boss fight with one hint |
| Boss | **Varro's war machine**: Phase 1 Lodestone the armour plates, Phase 2 Forge-heat the joints, Phase 3 roots and branches to reach the gear, Final one sling shot at Tavo's flaw (P6-B) |
| Release | Sunrise on the great oak; credits |

**Secrets**
- **6A Varro's Study**: Ogham *"The Prefect's Ambition"* (the villain's motive).
- **6B Armoury Vault**: Golden torc (unlocks the "Smith Brenna" costume).
- **6C Storm Spire**: the final shrine shard.

**Druid's Trial P6-T, The Last Trial:** one room that requires all five powers in a single sequence.
**Most memorable moment:** the final sling shot.
